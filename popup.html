Backstabbr Extras
  • Profile stats
    Per-country W/D/L, rates & highlights on member pages.
  • Illegal-move warnings
    Flags illegal orders on the game / sandbox board.
  • Filter history by country
    On a past turn, click countries in the map legend to show only their orders.
  • Press notes & pins
    Personal notes, pinned chats & per-chat notes in press.
  • Auto-save orders
    Auto-clicks “Update Orders” after you change a move. Saves the draft only — never marks you ready.
  • Game stats & export
    Adds a Stats tab: SC graph, score table, export (JSON/report) & import to sandbox.
  • Tab icons
    Shows an icon on each game tab: Orders, Press, Info, Sandboxes, Stats.
