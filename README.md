# Backstabbr Extras

A Chrome extension that adds useful things to [Backstabbr](https://www.backstabbr.com).
Toggle each feature on/off from the toolbar popup.

## Features

**Profile stats** — on a member profile, adds win/draw/loss rates, a per-country
record table (games played, W/D/L, win %, survival %), and highlights like your
most-played, strongest and weakest power.

**Illegal-move warnings** — on the board, outlines a province in red if you order
something illegal: a move to a province the unit can't reach, a bad coast, a support
into a province you're not next to, or a convoy that no fleet is actually carrying.

**Press notes & pins** — in a game's Press tab: a private notepad for the whole game,
pin important chats to the top, and private notes on each individual chat.

**Auto-save orders** — automatically clicks "Update Orders" a moment after you change
a move, so your orders are always saved. Only saves the draft, never marks you ready.
Off by default.

**Game stats & export** — adds a Stats tab with a supply-centre graph over the whole
game (hover for the numbers), a score table by year, and current standings. Export the
whole game as HTML or PDF, including every order of every turn and who played what.

**Themes** — recolour the whole site: Midnight, Crimson, Forest, Royal, Slate, Ember,
or the normal Backstabbr look. Plus a troll theme that makes everything go a bit crazy.

**Tab icons** — puts a small icon on each game tab (Results, Orders, Press, Info,
Sandboxes, Stats). Off by default.

Everything is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
